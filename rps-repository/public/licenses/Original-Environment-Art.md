# Original environment artwork

Six original environment images were generated specifically for Trinity: Afterlight using the OpenAI image generation tool during the October 2026 cinematic rebuild. They depict original scenery and were not downloaded from a third-party game, stock service, or asset pack. The prism observatory received a second generation-tool edit to remove the small human silhouettes from the architectural scene.

Each image retains its native 1672 × 941 resolution. Runtime WebP copies were encoded with FFmpeg libwebp at quality 94 without resizing or cropping. The generator PNG originals and an integrity manifest are retained in the development artifacts/environment-hd directory. The earlier six-scene atlas is retained there for provenance and is excluded from the runtime asset cache.
